About

Momina Athar, ACA — Chartered Accountant.

I'm Momina Athar, ACA, a Chartered Accountant. I built Accurao Reviewer as an independent review tool for finished UK small-company statutory accounts.

An independent check has to be independent of the thing being checked. That's why Accurao Reviewer does not prepare accounts: it reviews the finished accounts you've already produced.

Accurao Reviewer is pre-beta, built and tested, and not yet in general use.

Accurao Ltd also operates a bookkeeping and accounts practice under the Accurao name. Files submitted to Accurao Reviewer are used to run the review and are not used for that practice's client work or for business development.
