About Accurao Reviewer

Financial statement review, from trial balance to disclosure.

A connected review of the information behind the statements.

UK small-company statutory financial statements draw on information held across the trial balance, disclosure notes, supporting schedules and prior-year filed accounts. Reviewing each source separately can make gaps and inconsistencies difficult to see.

Accurao Reviewer brings those sources into one structured review. It supports disclosure verification under FRS 102 Section 1A, AI-assisted trial balance mapping, agreement of disclosure notes with underlying schedules, prior-year consistency checks using Companies House information, and casting and arithmetical checks.

Findings are returned for a qualified person to assess. The review remains human-led and professional judgement remains with the accountant or financial controller.

Focused by design

Accurao Reviewer reviews statutory financial statements and their supporting information. It does not prepare, edit, export or file accounts.

Current stage

The free invitation-only closed beta runs from 1 to 30 September 2026 with small accountancy practices, qualified accountants and financial controllers.

Accurao Reviewer does not perform an audit or assurance engagement, certify compliance, conclude on whether financial statements give a true and fair view, or replace accounts-production systems and professional judgement. It is developed and operated by Accurao Limited.
