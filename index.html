Reviewing the statements means looking beyond their face.

A financial statement review draws on information spread across the statements, disclosure notes, trial balance, supporting schedules and prior-year filed accounts.

Gaps can appear when a balance is not mapped into the statements, a disclosure note no longer agrees with its schedule, comparative information changes between periods, or a required disclosure is missing.

Accurao Reviewer brings those connections into one structured review and returns findings for a qualified person to assess.

What it checks

Disclosures and supporting evidence

Checks whether required information appears present and agrees with the information supporting it.

  • Disclosure verification and completeness — checks applicable FRS 102 Section 1A requirements and flags potential omissions for review
  • Note-to-schedule agreement — compares disclosure notes with their underlying schedules and surfaces differences
  • Identifier consistency — checks the company name, registered number and director names throughout the document

Figures and consistency

Connects the statements to their source information and checks whether the numbers hold together.

  • AI-assisted trial balance mapping — maps the trial balance to the financial statements to identify potential gaps and omissions
  • Prior-year consistency — retrieves prior-year statutory accounts from Companies House and compares current and comparative information
  • Casting and arithmetical accuracy — checks totals, subtotals, roll-ups and note-to-face agreement

Working through it

  • Materiality threshold you set
  • Confirm, dismiss or correct each finding
  • Per-section sign-off with an audit trail
  • Read-only view of the accounts PDF with findings anchored to the relevant text

It won't catch everything, and it doesn't replace your review. It catches the class of error that survives a careful read.

Financial statements in. Structured findings out. Your judgement stays in the loop.

It sits beside the tools you already use. Nothing is installed and nothing is written back into the accounts production workflow.

01

Provide the financial statements and supporting information

Upload the statutory financial statements as a text-readable PDF, together with the trial balance and relevant underlying schedules. Prior-year filed accounts are retrieved from Companies House.

02

Accurao Reviewer connects and checks the information

It reviews disclosure completeness, maps the trial balance to the statements, agrees notes to schedules, compares prior-year information and checks casting and arithmetical accuracy.

03

You work through the findings

Everything above the materiality threshold you set, anchored to the relevant text. Confirm, dismiss, or correct each one. Sign off section by section. The trail is kept.

Accurao Reviewer does not prepare, edit, export or file statutory accounts. It does not produce iXBRL, perform an audit or assurance engagement, certify compliance, or replace accounts-production systems and professional judgement. The financial statement view is read-only.
