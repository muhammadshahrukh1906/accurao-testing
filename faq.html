FAQ

Five questions, answered plainly.

Is my client data safe?

You should assess the actual controls rather than rely on a badge. Access is per-user and enforced at the database level, so information uploaded under one firm's login is not visible to users at another firm.

Before beta access, participants receive storage-region, AI-processing and sub-processor information. Uploads and results are retained during participation and for 30 days afterwards; residual backup copies are removed within the normal 90-day backup cycle. Accurao Reviewer is not currently ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certified. We will complete a security questionnaire before access where required.

Does it replace my review?

No, and it isn't built to. It performs a defined set of arithmetic and consistency checks and assesses whether certain disclosures appear present. It does not exercise professional judgement, assess whether an accounting treatment is appropriate, or form a view on whether the accounts give a true and fair view. It makes no compliance guarantee.

Your review is still your review. This narrows what it has to catch.

What files do I need?

Provide the statutory financial statements as a text-readable PDF. Add the underlying trial balance where trial balance mapping is required, together with the relevant supporting schedules for note-to-schedule checks.

Prior-year statutory accounts are retrieved directly from Companies House. A scanned or photographed PDF without extractable text is outside the current scope; PDFs exported from accounts-production systems are suitable.

Which accounts-production systems does it work with?

Accurao Reviewer works from the exported financial statements rather than integrating with the system that produced them. You provide a PDF from Caseware, IRIS, TaxCalc or another accounts-production system, together with the supporting information required for the selected checks.

Nothing is installed and nothing is written back into the accounts-production workflow.

What does it cost?

The invitation-only beta runs from 1 to 30 September 2026 and is completely free. Participation will not automatically convert into a paid service. Any future pricing will be communicated and agreed separately.
