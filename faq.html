FAQ

Six questions, answered plainly.

Is my client data safe?

The honest answer is that you should read this page and decide, not take a badge at face value.

Uploaded documents and trial balances are stored in {{DATA_REGION}} and retained for {{RETENTION}}, after which they're deleted. Access is per-user and enforced at the database level — accounts uploaded under your login are not visible to other users of the product, including other users at other firms.

The AI-assisted disclosure checks send document text to Anthropic's API. Anthropic does not train models on API inputs. Our sub-processors are {{SUBPROCESSORS}}.

We are not ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certified. We're a small closed beta and we'd rather say so than imply otherwise. If your firm requires a completed security questionnaire before you can use a third-party tool with client data, raise it on the call and we'll complete yours.

Does it replace my review?

No, and it isn't built to. It performs a defined set of arithmetic and consistency checks and assesses whether certain disclosures appear present. It does not exercise professional judgement, assess whether an accounting treatment is appropriate, or form a view on whether the accounts give a true and fair view. It makes no compliance guarantee.

Your review is still your review. This narrows what it has to catch.

What files do I need?

Two, minimum:

  • The finished accounts as a PDF
  • The trial balance they were prepared from ({{TB_FORMATS}})

Optionally, a fixed asset register ({{FAR_FORMATS}}) to cross-match against the fixed-asset note, and the prior-year accounts — though those can be fetched from Companies House.

One limitation worth knowing before you upload: the PDF needs to contain real text. A PDF that's been scanned or photographed contains no extractable text, and checks that depend on reading the document won't run against it. Accounts exported directly from your production software are fine.

What software does it work with?

All of it, because it doesn't integrate with any of it. You export the finished accounts as a PDF from whatever you already use — Caseware, IRIS, TaxCalc, or anything else — and the trial balance from your bookkeeping system. Nothing is installed, nothing changes in your existing workflow, and nothing is written back.

That's the point of it being independent. A check performed inside the tool that produced the figures shares that tool's assumptions.

Accurao also does bookkeeping. Do you compete with me?

Fair question, and the answer is partly yes, so here it is plainly. {{ENTITY}} also operates a bookkeeping practice serving small companies directly. It's a separate part of the business with its own website.

What that means in practice: we do not use anything uploaded to the Reviewer to identify, contact, or solicit your clients, and we don't market bookkeeping services to Reviewer users. If you'd rather have that as a written commitment before uploading anything, ask and we'll put it in the beta agreement.
