How it works

Accurao Reviewer sits beside the accounts production process. You give it the finished output, it runs a defined set of checks, and you work through the findings.

01

Give it the finished accounts and the trial balance

The signed or draft accounts as a PDF, and the trial balance they were prepared from. Optionally a fixed asset register, and last year's filed accounts — or let it pull those from Companies House.

02

It checks the accounts against the numbers, and against themselves

Every FS line tied back to the trial balance. The balance sheet foots. Notes agree to the face. The fixed asset note casts and matches the register. Comparatives match what was filed. Company name, registered number and director names used consistently throughout.

03

You work through the findings

Everything above the materiality threshold you set, anchored to the relevant text in the document. Confirm, dismiss, or correct each one. Sign off section by section. The trail is kept.

What you need

Two, minimum:

  • The finished accounts as a PDF
  • The trial balance they were prepared from ({{TB_FORMATS}})

Optionally, a fixed asset register ({{FAR_FORMATS}}) to cross-match against the fixed-asset note, and the prior-year accounts — though those can be fetched from Companies House.

One limitation worth knowing before you upload: the PDF needs to contain real text. A PDF that's been scanned or photographed contains no extractable text, and checks that depend on reading the document won't run against it. Accounts exported directly from your production software are fine.

It does not prepare, edit, export or file statutory accounts. There is no iXBRL output and no filing capability. It reviews the finished accounts you've already produced, in whatever software you produce them in. The document view is read-only, deliberately.
