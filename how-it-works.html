01

Provide the financial statements and supporting information

Upload the statutory financial statements as a text-readable PDF, together with the trial balance and relevant underlying schedules. Accurao Reviewer retrieves the prior-year statutory accounts filed at Companies House.

02

Accurao Reviewer connects and checks the information

It reviews disclosure completeness, maps the trial balance to the statements, agrees notes with supporting schedules, compares current and prior-year information, and checks casting and arithmetical accuracy.

03

You work through the findings

Everything above the materiality threshold you set, anchored to the relevant text in the document. Confirm, dismiss, or correct each one. Sign off section by section. The trail is kept.

What you need

  • The statutory financial statements as a text-readable PDF
  • The underlying trial balance, where trial balance mapping is required
  • The relevant supporting schedules for note-to-schedule checks

Prior-year statutory accounts are retrieved directly from Companies House. A scanned or photographed PDF without extractable text is outside the current scope; PDFs exported from accounts-production systems are suitable.

Accurao Reviewer does not prepare, edit, export or file statutory accounts. It does not produce iXBRL, perform an audit or assurance engagement, certify compliance, or replace accounts-production systems and professional judgement. The financial statement view is read-only.
