What it checks

The complete review is split deliberately between deterministic arithmetic and matching, and AI-assisted disclosure presence detection. They are not the same kind of finding.

✓ Exact checksArithmetic & tie-outs
AI-assisted checksDisclosure checks
AI
Designed to catch what slips through — across numbers and disclosures.

Exact checks

Arithmetic and matching. Deterministic — same inputs, same result, every time.

  • Trial balance tie-out — every line in the accounts traced back to the trial balance, with the difference shown
  • Internal consistency — the balance sheet foots, notes agree to the face of the balance sheet, the fixed asset note casts
  • Fixed asset register cross-match — your uploaded register against the fixed-asset note
  • Prior-year comparatives — checked against the actual prior-year accounts filed at Companies House, or a copy you upload
  • Identifier consistency — company name, registered number and director names used consistently throughout the document

AI-assisted checks

Presence detection, not certainty. Treat these as prompts to confirm.

  • Disclosure assessment — {{DISCLOSURE_COUNT}} FRS 102 Section 1A disclosure requirements, each returned with the specific text found in your document as evidence, so you can judge it yourself rather than trust a tick

Working through it

  • Materiality threshold you set
  • Confirm, dismiss or correct each finding
  • Per-section sign-off with an audit trail
  • Read-only view of the accounts PDF with findings anchored to the relevant text

It won't catch everything, and it doesn't replace your review. It catches the class of error that survives a careful read.

It does not prepare, edit, file, or export statutory accounts. It reviews the accounts you've already produced, in whatever software you produce them in. The document view is read-only, deliberately.
